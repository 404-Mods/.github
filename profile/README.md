## What we do

Everything we release fits one of three lanes.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **01 · Development** | Original mods, scripts, tools and systems — gameplay, maps and world, UI and HUD. Most of it in GTA, and not only there. |
| **02 · Projects & releases** | Public releases, development previews and updates — announced in the Discord first, then on the site. |
| **03 · Experimentation** | Not every idea needs to ship. We try concepts and systems just to see what works. |

## Open source

Some of what we build for ourselves is useful on its own. When it is, we put it here.

### [Headless Kit](https://github.com/404-Mods/Headless-Kit)

A self-hosted storefront for [Tebex](https://tebex.io), built on the Headless API — your own
domain, your own design, Tebex still doing checkout. Catalogue, cart, discount and creator
codes, account linking, reviews and theming, deployed to Cloudflare Workers.

[**Live demo**](https://headless-kit.404development.com) · [**Source**](https://github.com/404-Mods/Headless-Kit) · Next.js · TypeScript · Cloudflare Workers

## How we work

**Quality over quantity.** We'd rather take the time to create something polished than rush a project out the door.

**Attention to detail.** Projects released under 404 are built around thoughtful design and experiences that feel complete and refined.

**Always improving.** We're always learning, experimenting, and improving. Feedback from our community plays an important role in that process.

## What's here, and what isn't

Most of our work stays private while it's in development — mods in progress, the site, internal
tooling. What stands on its own gets opened up and lands in this org. Everything we've shipped is
listed on the site, with builds and changelogs under [Downloads](https://404development.com/downloads).
